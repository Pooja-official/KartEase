# KartEase — Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window
- Electronics (phones, laptops, tablets, smart watches, speakers): 10 days from delivery.
- Home and kitchen products: 30 days from delivery.
- Clothing and footwear: 30 days from delivery, with original tags attached.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Earphones and earbuds, for hygiene reasons (unless they arrive damaged or defective).
- Software, gift cards and digital downloads.
- Products marked "Non-returnable" on the product page.

## How to return
1. Go to My Orders, choose the item and click "Return".
2. Pick a reason and a pickup slot. Pickup is free.
3. Keep the product in its original box with all accessories.

## Damaged or wrong item
Report a damaged, defective or wrong item within 48 hours of delivery and upload unboxing photos. These cases get a free replacement, or a refund if no replacement is available.

## Refunds
- The refund starts after the returned item passes a quality check at our warehouse, usually within 2 days of pickup.
- Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after the quality check.
- For Cash on Delivery orders, the refund goes to your bank account or UPI ID within 5 to 7 working days.
- Delivery charges are refunded only if the item was damaged, defective or wrong.
