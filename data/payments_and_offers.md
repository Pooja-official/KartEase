# KartEase — Payments and Offers Policy

## Payment methods
- UPI, credit cards, debit cards and net banking.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for orders up to ₹10,000.

## EMI
- No-cost EMI is available on orders of ₹3,000 or more with selected bank credit cards, for 3 or 6 months.
- Standard EMI with interest is available for 9 and 12 months.

## Coupons and bank offers
- Only one coupon can be used per order.
- Coupons cannot be combined with bank offers on the same order.
- If an order with a coupon is returned, the coupon is not given back.

## KartEase SuperCoins
- You earn 1 SuperCoin for every ₹100 spent on delivered orders.
- 1 SuperCoin is worth ₹1 and can pay for up to 20% of an order.
- SuperCoins expire 12 months after they are earned.
