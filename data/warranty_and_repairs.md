# KartEase — Warranty and Repairs Policy

## Manufacturer warranty
- Most electronics come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the date of delivery.
- Laptops from some brands come with a 2-year warranty. The product page shows the exact warranty period.
- Warranty claims are handled by the brand's authorised service centre. Keep your KartEase invoice, which you can download from My Orders.

## Dead on arrival
If a product does not switch on or work when it arrives, report it within 7 days of delivery. KartEase replaces dead-on-arrival products directly, without sending you to the service centre.

## KartEase Extended Protection
- Adds 1 extra year of cover after the manufacturer warranty ends.
- Can be bought only within 30 days of delivery.
- Price: 8% of the product price, with a minimum of ₹299.

## Not covered
Physical damage, liquid damage, damage from unauthorised repairs, and normal wear and tear are not covered by the warranty or by Extended Protection.
