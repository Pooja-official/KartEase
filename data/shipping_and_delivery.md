# KartEase — Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Delivery charges
- Free delivery on orders of ₹499 or more.
- Orders below ₹499 have a delivery charge of ₹40.
- Express delivery (next day) costs ₹99 and is available in 12 metro cities, including Chennai, Coimbatore, Bengaluru and Hyderabad.

## Delivery time
- Metro cities: 3 to 5 working days.
- Other cities and towns: 5 to 8 working days.
- Orders placed before 2 PM are dispatched the same day. Orders after 2 PM are dispatched the next working day.

## Tracking
You get a tracking link by SMS and email once the order is shipped. Tracking can also be checked in My Orders.

## Address changes and failed deliveries
- The delivery address can be changed only before the order is shipped.
- The courier makes 3 delivery attempts. After 3 failed attempts the order is returned to us and a refund is started.

## Cancellation
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they are shipped.
- After shipping, refuse the delivery or raise a return instead.
